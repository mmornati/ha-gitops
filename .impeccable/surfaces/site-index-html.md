# Surface: HA GitOps marketing page (GitHub Pages)

Scope: `site/index.html`, published at https://mmornati.github.io/ha-gitops/. Visitor mode: Persuade. Inherits the Strip Bay world from DESIGN.md unchanged.

Audience and job: Home Assistant owners who keep, or want to keep, their config in git. They must understand in seconds what the app does, believe that nothing reaches their base branch unreviewed and that secrets stay home, and add the repository to Home Assistant.

Proof on hand: the real panel (screenshots in both themes, desktop and phone), the real workflow, the real option names. No testimonials, numbers or logos exist and none are invented.

Constraints: static, no build step, no external requests except the my.home-assistant.io links; English; code-led.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Two Sides of the Wire. The page is split like the panel's head rule, the box on the left and GitHub on the right, and a change is shown crossing the wire and coming back. Refuses the feature-grid landing page.

OWN-WORLD: DESIGN.md as built: sage or green-black bay, paper strips with flat state fields, keys on rails, monospace only for paths, commits and code.

STORY: they see a file edited on the box become a pull request, pass CI, get merged and come back applied, while a secret-looking file never leaves; they learn how to configure it; they press Add to Home Assistant.

FIRST VIEWPORT: a short headline and one sentence at left with the install key and the video key; directly under, the head rule with its state field and the two columns of the live demo, a strip already on the box side. The primary action is the paper key in the hero and again in the install section.

SIGNATURE INTERACTION: the demo's strip travels across the centre wire as the steps advance (view transition), the state field changing word and colour; steps can be driven by hand. MOTION GRAMMAR: state changes only, the panel's ease, nothing on scroll.

FORM: split page, third of seven structural candidates. Seed key 2478740d.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
